〰️ Frequency Tone Generator

Generate a steady tone or a frequency sweep to explore audio frequencies and check what your playback device can reproduce. Runs locally in your browser.

Playback range depends on your browser and audio device.

Important limits

This is a tone generator, not a proven pest repellent. Scientific sources have not established a reliably effective ultrasonic frequency for repelling household pests. A browser can request a frequency, but your device may filter or fail to reproduce it; this tool cannot measure the sound coming from your speaker.

Start at low volume, keep speakers away from ears, and stop if the sound is uncomfortable. High-frequency sounds may still be audible or bothersome to children and animals. Do not use this tool as pest control.
