〰️ Frequency Tone Generator

Generate a steady tone or a frequency sweep to explore audio frequencies. Runs locally in your browser; it cannot generate ultrasonic sound.

Playback range depends on your browser and audio device.

Important limits

This is a tone generator, not a proven rat repellent. Its output is capped at 20,000 Hz, while ultrasound starts above 20,000 Hz. Rats can hear well into the ultrasonic range, so this browser tool cannot test the frequencies often discussed in rat studies. A browser can request a frequency, but your device may filter or fail to reproduce it; this tool cannot measure the sound coming from your speaker.

Start at low volume, keep speakers away from ears, and stop if the sound is uncomfortable. High-frequency sounds may still be audible or bothersome to children and animals. Do not use this tool as pest control.

What rat studies found

Rat hearing extends beyond the range this browser tool can produce. In a field study, a narrow-band device near 22 kHz initially reduced rat feeding in the treated room, but feeding rose again after one week; a broad-band device peaking near 41 kHz did not measurably reduce activity in that room. Those results do not identify a reliably effective setting for a household speaker.

A credible test would need an ultrasonic transducer and amplifier, calibrated ultrasonic measurement equipment, and repeated on/off observations of rat activity in the actual space. Frequency and sound pressure both matter, and one short playback cannot establish lasting repellency.

Sources: Norway rat audiogram · field test of 22 kHz and 41 kHz devices · controlled and field testing protocols
