Why remove links from a PDF?

A PDF can carry invisible clickable areas. Share it and someone taps a line of text expecting nothing to happen — and lands on a website you never chose. Question banks, textbooks, invoices and downloaded forms often come with them built in.

This tool removes those annotation objects and leaves the page itself completely alone: no re-rendering, no flattening, no image conversion. The text stays selectable, the fonts and diagrams stay sharp, and the layout cannot shift. The page count and the document's title/author metadata are kept too.

Links only is the safe default — it removes the clickable areas and keeps your own comments and highlights. Choose Everything if you also want comments, sticky notes, highlights, stamps and form fields gone.
